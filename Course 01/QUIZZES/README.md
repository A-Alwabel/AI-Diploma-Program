# Quizzes Overview
## AIAT 111 - Introduction to Artificial Intelligence and Applications

There are five quizzes, one per unit. The five quiz files are in this folder, one per unit, as in every other course. Take each quiz after finishing that unit's examples and exercise.

| Quiz | Unit | Location | What it assesses |
|------|------|----------|------------------|
| Quiz 01 | Unit 1 — Introduction to AI and Applications | `quiz_01.md` | What AI is, narrow vs general AI, the field's history and applications, and a rule-based recommender |
| Quiz 02 | Unit 2 — AI Concepts, Terminology, and Application Domains | `quiz_02.md` | Search algorithms: BFS, DFS, A* and heuristics, with a BFS implementation |
| Quiz 03 | Unit 3 — AI Concepts, Terminology, and Application Domains Part 2 | `quiz_03.md` | Machine learning basics, from the printed results of notebooks 01–05: regression vs classification and their metrics, the perceptron and XOR, training a Keras network, gradient descent and the learning rate, MSE vs cross-entropy, and SHAP/LIME explanations |
| Quiz 04 | Unit 4 — Neural Networks Fundamentals | `quiz_04.md` | Perceptrons, activation functions, forward propagation, and a perceptron for the AND gate |
| Quiz 05 | Unit 5 — Introduction to Generative AI and Course Summary | `quiz_05.md` | Generative vs discriminative models and the course summary |

Answer keys are released by your instructor.
