# Assessments - AIAT 112

Assessment materials for Python for Artificial Intelligence.

---

## Files in This Folder

- `Final_Exam.md` - final exam for the course.
- `Project_Rubric.md` - rubric for evaluating course projects.
- `Notebook_Assessment_Rubric.md` - rubric for evaluating notebook work.

## Related Materials

- **Quizzes:** `../QUIZZES/` - six quizzes (Quiz_00 .. Quiz_05); see
  `../QUIZZES/README.md` for when to take each one.
- **Projects:** `../PROJECTS/` - three projects; complete at least one.
- **Self-assessment:** `../SELF_ASSESSMENT/` - optional checkpoints.

---

## How Assessment Works

- Take each quiz after finishing the corresponding unit (Quiz_00 follows the
  first notebook of Unit 1).
- Projects are evaluated with `Project_Rubric.md`; read it before submitting.
- Notebook work is evaluated with `Notebook_Assessment_Rubric.md`.
- Grade weights and the assessment schedule are set by your instructor.

Answer keys and solutions are released by your instructor - they are not
distributed with the student materials.
