# Unit 4: Ethical and Regulatory Considerations

## AIAT 124 - Generative Artificial Intelligence

Unit training hours: 14

## What This Unit Teaches

The ethical, legal, and social side of generative AI: sources of bias and how
to detect and mitigate it, deepfakes and misinformation, intellectual property
questions, and regulatory frameworks such as GDPR and the EU AI Act. The
notebooks are hands-on: bias measurement with scikit-learn, a deepfake
detection workflow, and applying regulatory guidelines to model development.

## Prerequisites

- Units 1–3 completed (you should understand what generative models can
  produce and where their risks come from)
