# Self-Assessment Materials

This folder contains self-assessment checkpoints to help students track their progress through Course 04.

---

## Purpose

Self-assessment checkpoints help you:
- ✅ Verify your understanding after each module/unit
- ✅ Identify areas that need review
- ✅ Track your learning progress
- ✅ Prepare for quizzes and projects
- ✅ Build confidence in your knowledge

---

## How to Use

1. **After completing each module/unit:**
   - Complete the relevant checkpoint
   - Be honest about your understanding
   - Review areas where you're weak
   - Don't move forward until you're ready

2. **Before starting projects:**
   - Complete final checkpoint
   - Assess your readiness
   - Review weak areas
   - Plan your project approach

## Tips

1. **Be Honest:** Don't check boxes you're not sure about
2. **Review Regularly:** Use checkpoints after each module
3. **Take Action:** Review weak areas before moving on
4. **Ask for Help:** Use checkpoints to identify what to ask about
