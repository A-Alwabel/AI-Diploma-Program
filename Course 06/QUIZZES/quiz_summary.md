## Format

Each quiz: **30 minutes**, **30 marks**, English. Multiple choice, short answer and case analysis.

---

## How to use them

1. Work through the unit's examples and exercise first.
2. Review the unit material.
3. Take the quiz closed-book — that is the point of it.
4. **Answer keys are released by your instructor.** They are not in this repository.
5. Go back to the lessons behind anything you got wrong, not to the key.
