# Unit 5: Project Documentation and Final Presentation

Unit hours: 17 total (3 theory + 14 practical)

You write the final project report, prepare your presentation and demo, and assemble the complete submission package.

## Builds on (AIAT 111-125)

**Builds on:** AIAT 116 U4/U5 (accountability frameworks, model cards, regulation) · AIAT 125 U5 (versioning and reproducibility, so your demo runs on the reviewer's machine) · AIAT 115 U3 (figures an audience can read) · AIAT 111 U5 (explaining an AI system to a non-specialist).

Every artifact you reuse goes in **section 6, the Prior Work Inventory**, of [`TEMPLATES/project_proposal_template.md`](../TEMPLATES/project_proposal_template.md) — scored at gate 1 and re-checked at the design review. It is this course's **CLO2** evidence: AIAT 126 integrates the diploma, it does not re-teach it.
