# Unit 1: Project Proposal and Plan

Unit hours: 14 total (4 theory + 10 practical)

You choose your graduation project, research the background, and write a proposal with a concrete plan: goals, methodology, success metrics, timeline, and resources.

## Builds on (AIAT 111-125)

**Builds on:** AIAT 111 U2 (matching a technique to a problem domain) · AIAT 115 U1 (framing a question the data can answer) · AIAT 116 U5 (regulation and permitted use, for your dataset licence) · AIAT 113 U5 (reading a paper's numbers critically).

Every artifact you reuse goes in **section 6, the Prior Work Inventory**, of [`TEMPLATES/project_proposal_template.md`](../TEMPLATES/project_proposal_template.md) — scored at gate 1 and re-checked at the design review. It is this course's **CLO2** evidence: AIAT 126 integrates the diploma, it does not re-teach it.
