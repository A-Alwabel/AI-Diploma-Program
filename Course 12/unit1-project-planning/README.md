# Unit 1: Project Proposal and Plan

Unit hours: 14 total (4 theory + 10 practical)

You choose your graduation project, research the background, and write a proposal with a concrete plan: goals, methodology, success metrics, timeline, and resources.

## Prerequisites

- Semester 1 (AIAT 111-116) and Semester 2 (AIAT 121-125)
- Read [../START_HERE.md](../START_HERE.md) and [../PROJECT_GUIDELINES.md](../PROJECT_GUIDELINES.md)
