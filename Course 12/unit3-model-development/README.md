# Unit 3: Implementation and Development of the Project Idea

Unit hours: 17 total (3 theory + 14 practical)

You build your project: collect and prepare the data, implement the system you designed in Unit 2, and train and tune your models.

## Builds on (AIAT 111-125)

**Builds on:** AIAT 115 U2 (cleaning — this unit does **not** re-teach it) · AIAT 114 U1/U2/U5 (preprocessing, cross-validation, grid search) · AIAT 111 U3 (gradient descent and loss functions) · AIAT 116 U3 (what you may legally keep) · AIAT 125 U5 (experiment tracking).

Every artifact you reuse goes in **section 6, the Prior Work Inventory**, of [`TEMPLATES/project_proposal_template.md`](../TEMPLATES/project_proposal_template.md) — scored at gate 1 and re-checked at the design review. It is this course's **CLO2** evidence: AIAT 126 integrates the diploma, it does not re-teach it.

## Prerequisites

- Unit 2: Solution System Design and Architecture (a system design for your project)
