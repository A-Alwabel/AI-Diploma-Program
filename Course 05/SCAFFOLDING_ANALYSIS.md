---

## What is Scaffolding?

**Scaffolding** in educational notebooks refers to **BEFORE/AFTER sections** that help students understand:
- **BEFORE**: What they know/don't know before starting
- **AFTER**: What they'll know/be able to do after completing the notebook

**Example from Course 02:**
```markdown
**BEFORE**: We have scattered information - "Socrates is human", "Humans are mortal" - but no way to connect them.

**AFTER**: We'll build a knowledge system that can store facts, find connections, and even **INFER** new knowledge!
```

1. **✅ Simplest, clearest teaching**

2. **✅ Student learning focus**
   - Research shows scaffolding improves learning outcomes
   - Helps students understand **why** they're learning something
   - Connects new knowledge to existing knowledge

3. **✅ Curriculum alignment**
   - Scaffolding can reference **DETAILED_UNIT_DESCRIPTIONS.md** learning objectives
   - Makes curriculum goals visible to students

## How to Add Scaffolding

**Template:**
```markdown
## The Story | القصة

**BEFORE**: [What students know/don't know before]
- Current state
- What's missing
- Why it matters

**AFTER**: [What students will know/do after]
- New capabilities
- What they'll be able to do
- How it connects to next steps
```

**Where to add:**
- After "Learning Objectives" section
- Before main content starts
- In key transition notebooks (first in unit, complex topics)
