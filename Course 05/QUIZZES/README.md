## Quiz Format

- **Time limit:** 30 minutes

- **Format:** multiple choice, code writing, short answer

## How to Use

1. Complete the unit's examples and exercise first
2. Review the unit content
3. Take the quiz without referring to materials
4. Check your answers against the answer key when it is released by your instructor
5. Review the areas where you scored low

Answer keys are released by your instructor.
