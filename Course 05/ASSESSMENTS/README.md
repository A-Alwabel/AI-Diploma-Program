# Course 05 Assessments

## Final Exam

- **File:** `Final_Exam.md`
- **Time limit:** 2 hours
- **Total points:** 100 points
- **Format:** 13 questions in 5 parts — multiple choice (24), short answer (27), coding (24), case study (15), evaluation and monitoring (10)

The multiple-choice section is not a warm-up. Every option is a claim someone could
plausibly hold, so the way through Part 1 is to know which lesson refutes which option.
Five of the six items hand you numbers a lesson actually printed — timings, call counts,
skew values — and ask what follows from them; the sixth puts you in a scenario and asks
which tool fits. No option can be struck out on its wording alone.

Take the final exam after completing all five units, their exercises, and the unit quizzes in `../QUIZZES/`.

Answer keys are released by your instructor.
